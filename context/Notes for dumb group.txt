3. Payment Process

- Chooses a reservation type (Ocular Visit, Resort)
- Customer see's calendar and picks a date 
- Chooses from 3 Cards (Day Tour, 22 Hours, Overnight)
- Customer fills out form:

a. First Name
b. Last Name
c. (Optional) Company Name
d. Phone Number
e. Guest Count
f. Special Event

- Customer Submits Form
- Customer confirms reservation
- Customer Pays in GCash
- Customer Sends Proof of Payment
- Admin Confirms payment received
- Admin Confirms Reservation
- Admin Sends Printable Receipt 
- Customer prints Receipt
- Customer present Receipt at the resort 
- Finished
